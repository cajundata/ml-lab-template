- **The smoke data is linearly separable by construction, so the model nails it.** Near-perfect metrics here mean *the lifecycle ran*, not *the model is good*. Do not read them as a quality signal, and do not be alarmed when Phase 1's real numbers are far worse.
- **`train-smoke` reports `train_accuracy` / `train_f1` — scored on the training split itself.** That is intentional (it is a smoke signal, not a generalization estimate), but it is exactly the kind of metric that gets misread once real data arrives.
- **`vllm_smoke` fails and is expected to.** vLLM does not serve on this stack; the probe is *informational* and deliberately excluded from the required set. Do not "fix" the pipeline by promoting it — it belongs to the Phase-5 cloud-serving track.
- **The run-id handoff is a file on disk.** `evaluate-smoke` finds its MLflow run via `reports/smoke/latest/train_run_id.txt`. `make clean` deletes that file, so a `clean` between train and evaluate breaks the chain (loudly, at least — it raises rather than opening a new run).
- The two required GPU probes overlap: `transformers_smoke` exercises the same torch/CUDA path `torch_cuda` already proved. That redundancy is deliberate — one proves the device works, the other proves a real model workload runs on it.
